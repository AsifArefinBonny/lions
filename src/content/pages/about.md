Lions Club of Dhaka Oasis was chartered on 29 August 1997. The club was founded by Ln. Sheikh Anisur Rahman PMJF and belongs to Lions International, District 315 A2, Bangladesh.

Since then our members have given their time to service: eye camps and cataract surgery, diabetes camps, blood donation, tree plantation, and the distribution of education materials and food. We sponsor the Leo Club of Dhaka Oasis to help young people grow into leaders.

We meet every month, and each meeting is reported in our monthly bulletin.
