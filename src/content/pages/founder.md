Ln. Sheikh Anisur Rahman PMJF is the founder of the Lions Club of Dhaka Oasis, which received its charter on 29 August 1997.

A Lion since 1988, he continues to guide the club. In Lions year 2025–2026 he served as Chairperson of the club's Membership & Advisory Committee.
